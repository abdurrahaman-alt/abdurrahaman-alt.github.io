Institute Single Line Diagram (SLD)

Emergency Maintenance & Electrical Layout Project

Overview & Objectives

Designed a comprehensive Single Line Diagram (SLD) for the institute's power distribution system to map high/low voltage lines, switchgears, transformers, and distribution panels for fast fault identification and emergency response.

Key Technical Components

  • Main Distribution Board (MDB) & Sub Distribution Board (SDB) mapping.
  • Load calculation and circuit breaker sizing (MCCB, MCB, RCCB).
  • Transformer, HT/LT Panel, and Cable Routing Diagrams.
  • Compliance with BNBC & Electrical Safety Codes.
