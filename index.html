About

Hello, I'm Abdur Rahaman.

I completed my Diploma-in-Engineering in Electrical Technology from Comilla Polytechnic Institute under the Bangladesh Technical Education Board, graduating with a final CGPA of 3.90 out of 4.00.

My training spans industrial robot operation and troubleshooting, PLC, HMI, VFD and SCADA-based control systems, and 2D/3D CAD drafting for electrical, mechanical, civil and architectural layouts. I've also completed structured training in industrial attachment and computer operations.

On the project side, I built an IoT-based home automation system with multiple control modes and redesigned an institute Single Line Diagram (SLD) for emergency maintenance — work that placed 3rd in an institute-level skills competition.

I'm now looking to grow into roles in electrical design, industrial automation, and maintenance, where I can apply both my technical training and hands-on project experience.
